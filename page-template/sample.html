What To Do Before Your Plumber Arrives

To minimise property damage while our Cameron Park plumber is booking it to your location:

  • Shut off the mains Locate your water meter (usually near the front boundary) and turn the valve clockwise.
  • Drain the lines Open the lowest tap on your property to let excess water drain out safely.
  • Power down If water is near electrical outlets, turn off the power at the main switchboard safely.

Do you handle emergency plumbing repairs in Cameron Park?

Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency plumbing across Cameron Park for burst pipes, major leaks, blocked drains, overflowing toilets and hot water failures.

What should we do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off the water at the main valve, usually near the front boundary, then switch off power if water is near outlets and call us.

Is a gurgling drain a sign of a blockage?

Gurgling often means air is trapped by a partial blockage. Getting it checked early can stop the drain backing up or overflowing inside the home.

How much does an after-hours call out cost?

The cost depends on the time of day, the fault, access and the materials needed. We will talk you through it when you call.
